Terms of Use

Updated October 25, 2026

Who can use Local Climb

Local Climb is a community app for climbers at CRG Framingham. You must be 13 or older. You sign in with Apple and are responsible for what happens on your account.

Climbing is dangerous

Climbing can cause serious injury. Local Climb doesn't supervise climbing, check routes or make anything safe. Never climb, film or witness in a way that puts you or others at risk, and follow the gym's rules and staff at all times.

What you post

Problem names, photos, videos, your username, your climbing level, votes and reports are visible to other signed-in climbers. You keep ownership of what you post and give Local Climb permission to store and show it in the app. Only post what you have the right to share, and only film people who agree to be filmed.

Zero tolerance for objectionable content

No hateful, sexual, violent, harassing or bullying content, no names that target a person, no impersonation and no spam. Objectionable content and abusive users aren't tolerated. Admins review reports within 24 hours, remove content that breaks these rules, and remove access for people who break them.

Reporting and blocking

You can report any name, video, problem or climber, and block any climber. Blocking hides their name and videos from you and stops either of you witnessing the other's sends.

The naming game

Naming rules, timers and votes are enforced by the app and its server. Admins can remove a name or reset a wall. Names are a community tradition, not a prize, and can be removed if they break these terms.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in Settings at any time. Your sends, videos, votes and blocks are deleted, and any pending name is removed. Locked names stay in the gym's history without your name on them.

Changes

These terms may change. If they change in a way that matters, the app will ask you to accept them again. Questions: contact the admins, using Report in the app, or the support page at https://nathaniel-feldman.github.io/localclimb/support.
