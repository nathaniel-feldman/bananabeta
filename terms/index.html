Terms of Use

Updated November 22, 2026

Who can use Banana Beta

Banana Beta is a community app for climbers at their gyms. You must be 13 or older. You sign in with Apple and are responsible for what happens on your account.

Climbing is dangerous

Climbing can cause serious injury. Banana Beta doesn't supervise climbing, check routes or make anything safe. Never climb, film or witness in a way that puts you or others at risk, and follow the gym's rules and staff at all times.

What you post

Problem names, photos, videos of your sends and falls, your username, your gym, a one-line bio if you write one, your height and wingspan if you add them, your rank, likes and comments are visible to other signed-in climbers. You keep ownership of what you post and give Banana Beta permission to store and show it in the app. Only post what you have the right to share, and only film people who agree to be filmed. A climb you post for a friend shows on their profile, and they can remove it.

Zero tolerance for objectionable content

No hateful, sexual, violent, harassing or bullying content, no names that target a person, no impersonation and no spam. Objectionable content and abusive users aren't tolerated. Admins review reports within 24 hours, remove content that breaks these rules, and remove access for people who break them.

Reporting and blocking

You can report any name, video, comment, problem or climber, and block any climber. A post reported by 3 different climbers is hidden until an admin looks at it. Blocking hides their name and videos from you and stops either of you witnessing the other's sends.

The naming game

Naming rules and timers are enforced by the app and its server. Admins can remove a name or reset a wall. Names are a community tradition, not a prize, and can be removed if they break these terms.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in Settings at any time. Your sends, falls, videos, votes and blocks are deleted, and any pending name is removed. Locked names stay in the gym's history without your name on them.

Changes

These terms may change. If they change in a way that matters, the app will ask you to accept them again. Questions: contact the admins, using Report in the app, or the support page at https://nathaniel-feldman.github.io/bananabeta/support.
