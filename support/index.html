Support

Something not working, or need a name, video or climber looked at? Use Report in the app: on a problem, a video or a climber's profile. Admins review reports within 24 hours.

To delete your account and everything you've posted: Profile → Settings → Delete account.

Questions: the admins, using Report in the app, or the support page at https://nathaniel-feldman.github.io/bananabeta/support.
