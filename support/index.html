Support

Something's wrong with a name, video or climber

Use Report in the app: on a problem, a video, a comment or a climber's profile. Admins review reports within 24 hours. You can also block anyone.

I have an idea, or found a bug

In the app: Profile → Settings → Suggest an idea or report a problem. It goes straight to the team.

How do I name a new problem?

Be the first to send it, and have a friend scan your code as witness (it shows right after you post). The first witnessed send gets to name it.

My gym isn't in the app

Open the gym picker and ask for it. We add gyms as climbers ask.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Settings → Delete account. It removes everything you've posted.

Anything else? Message us on Instagram, or use Report in the app and an admin will see it.
