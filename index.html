Banana Beta

Climbing reels for your gym.

Film your sends and your falls, see what climbers everywhere are sending, and earn your monkey one grade at a time.

  • Film it. Tap the mango, film, pick Sent it or Fell off and the climb you were on. Post in a few taps, or post one for a friend.
  • The Feed. Full-screen climbing videos: Following, My Gym, Everyone and Fails. Like, comment, and reply to comments.new
  • Ranks. Three different climbs at a grade or harder earn that grade's monkey, from Marmoset at VB to the Banana God at V17. A Gym Rank and a Board Rank, and they never go down.
  • Name new problems. Every new boulder starts out blank. Send it first, with a friend scanning your code as witness, and you get to name it.
  • The Banana Board. Log board climbs at any angle, holds lit the way your board lights them.
  • Your tree. Every climb you send hangs a banana on your profile, in the problem's color.
  • 88 gyms across the US so far. Add a problem to any wall, and ask for your gym if it isn't there yet.
  • Tell us. Got an idea or found a problem? Send it from Settings and it goes straight to the team.new
