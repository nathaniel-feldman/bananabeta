How it works

  1. 1

    Film your climb

    Tap the mango and film. Sent it or fell off, both count for something.

  2. 2

    Post it

    Pick the climb you were on and post in a few taps. Filmed a friend? Post it for them.

  3. 3

    Earn your monkey

    Three different climbs at a grade or harder and that grade's monkey is yours.

The Feed

Full-screen climbing videos: Following, My Gym, Everyone and Fails. Like, comment and reply.

Name new problems

Every new boulder starts out blank. Send it first, with a friend as witness, and you name it.

Your tree

Every climb you send hangs a banana on your profile, in the problem's color.

The Banana Board

Log board climbs at any angle, with the holds lit the way your board lights them.

Leaderboards

Your gym, the people you follow, and everyone. You stay in view even when you're far down.

88 gyms

Across the US so far. Add a problem to any wall, and ask for your gym if it isn't there yet.
