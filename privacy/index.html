Local Climb

Privacy

Updated October 25, 2026

What we collect

Your Sign in with Apple account ID and email (which can be Apple's private relay address), your name if you choose to share it (only to suggest a username), the username you pick, your climbing level, problem photos added before October 2026, send videos you film (video only, no audio), your sends, witness confirmations, votes, reports, blocks, whether you bought Support Local Climb (Apple handles the payment; we never see your card), and crash reports from the app.

Who sees it

Other signed-in climbers see usernames, climbing levels (shown as your monkey), problem names, send videos, ascents and vote totals. Who voted how is private. Reports and blocks are private; admins see reports.

Where it's stored

On Supabase servers in the United States. Problem photos and videos are in private storage and only shown to signed-in climbers. Profile pictures are drawn monkeys, so no photos of you are stored.

How long we keep it

Sends, videos and names stay while your account exists. A send you delete is kept 30 days so you can restore it, then it and its video are deleted. Crash reports are kept up to 90 days.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking across apps, no selling data, no location or microphone access.

Your choices

Change your level or username, or delete your account, in Settings. Deleting removes your sends, videos, votes and blocks. Questions: the admins, using Report in the app, or the support page at https://nathaniel-feldman.github.io/localclimb/support.
