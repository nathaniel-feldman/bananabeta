Privacy

Updated November 22, 2026

What we collect

Your Sign in with Apple account ID and email (which can be Apple's private relay address), your name if you choose to share it (only to suggest a username), the username you pick, your gym, your bio, your rank (worked out from your filmed sends), problem photos added before October 2026, send videos you film (with sound, if you allow the microphone), your sends and falls, your height and wingspan if you add them (optional, shown on your posts so climbers can find beta from people their size), likes, comments, gyms you ask us to add, witness confirmations, votes, reports, blocks, ideas and problems you send us from Settings, whether you bought Support Banana Beta (Apple handles the payment; we never see your card), your projects and attempt counts, the climbers you follow and your friends, climbs you post for a friend or a friend posts for you, Banana Board climbs you add, the app version you use (so old versions can be asked to update), crash reports from the app, and simple usage counts (when you open the app, share a clip, search, start a project, send, follow, vote or name, with no content), so the gym's admins can see how the app is used.

Who sees it

Other signed-in climbers see usernames, ranks (shown as your monkey), problem names, send and fall videos, ascents, comments, and your height and wingspan if you added them. You can hide yourself from leaderboards in Settings. Who voted how on 1.x names is private. Reports and blocks are private; admins see reports.

Where it's stored

On Supabase servers in the United States. Problem photos and videos are in private storage and only shown to signed-in climbers. Profile pictures are drawn monkeys, so no photos of you are stored.

How long we keep it

Sends, videos and names stay while your account exists. A send you delete is kept 30 days so you can restore it, then it and its video are deleted. Crash reports are kept up to 90 days, usage counts for a year.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking across apps, no selling data, no location access. The microphone is used only while you film, and only if you allow it.

Your choices

Change your username, remove your height and wingspan, hide from leaderboards, or delete your account, in Settings. Deleting removes your sends, falls, videos, likes, comments, votes and blocks. Questions: the admins, using Report in the app, or the support page at https://nathaniel-feldman.github.io/bananabeta/support.
